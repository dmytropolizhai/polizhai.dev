# Hero (home page, first viewport)

Mode: Experience. Extends the established Poster mono world (design/design-system.md); no DESIGN.md change.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints
- Clients and startups hiring a full-stack developer. Job: decide to get in touch.
- Proof: five live sites at equal weight: pro-lifts.lv, curio.today, zinly.polizhai.dev, stundio.lv, shop.numerologgi.com. Real screenshots in `public/shots/`.
- Hard rules: no game development; no lift/elevator metaphor in our own copy or decoration; no project singled out; new copy needs Dmytro's OK (Zinly card, 4 locales).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hero is the work itself. A full-bleed, freely draggable canvas of five real sites sits under one line of type, and nothing frames or explains it. It refuses the template hero of headline, subline, buttons and a screenshot strip.

OWN-WORLD: Poster mono. Archivo wide, black ground, white as the only accent, grey for hierarchy. Cards are rounded Liquid Glass slabs tilted on the black surface: white-tinted backdrop blur, a bright specular rim, a pointer-following glare, the real screenshot inset like a lens. The only colour on the page is the screenshots' own. Pills for controls, square everything else; the canvas keeps its named exceptions (tilt, free 2D drag, glass cards with rounded corners), now with no rounded frame.

STORY: Visitor reads "Make it run, make it right", sees real products peeking at the fold, drags, opens one, then taps "Let's talk". They believe the work is live and one person shipped it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band: H1 "Make it run, make it right." in the poster title scale, centred, static; one subline beneath; two pills, "See the work" (secondary) and "Let's talk" (primary). Under it the canvas starts and runs full-bleed to the bottom of the screen and past it (about 85svh tall), so five tilted screenshot cards peek at the fold. The ferrofluid stays behind the band. "See the work" scrolls the canvas to fill the viewport.

FORM: Established-world extension, no roll (surface scope not opened). Seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance. (DESIGN.md: unchanged by approval, extension of an established world; rasters are Dmytro's own site screenshots.)
